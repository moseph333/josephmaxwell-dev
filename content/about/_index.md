## Who I Am

I'm **Joseph Maxwell**, a Datacenter Technician at Google with hands-on experience building and maintaining the hyperscale infrastructure that powers AI and cloud computing at scale.

My work lives at the intersection of **physical hardware** and **systems operations** — I commission TPU accelerator clusters, isolate optical network faults, lead data sanitization operations, and build Go tools that eliminate technician toil.

Before Google, I spent 14 years in client-facing IT consultation and operations management, which gave me a foundation in clear communication, hardware servicing, and fast incident triage under pressure.

**AI/ML Infrastructure & Deployments**
Commission and maintain Google Cloud TPU accelerator clusters. Travel for multi-site deployments to handle physical installation, rack-level integration, and cluster turn-up.

**Optical Interconnects & Fiber Testing**
Isolate optical transport faults using power meters, Visual Fault Locators (VFL), and loopback plugs across fiber patch infrastructure and Optical Circuit Switches (OCS).

**Diagnostics & Defect Elimination**
Run root-cause failure analysis across 23+ production architectures (accelerator, compute, high-density storage). Update central repair documentation to eliminate recurring repair errors across the fleet.

**Data Security & Sanitization Leadership**
Directed site data sanitization operations as Primary Lead, enforcing NIST SP 800-88 compliance, secure storage rules, and physical destruction standards across hardware decommissioning and platform upgrades.

**Tooling & Logistics Automation**
Built an internal web-based logistics tool in Go and REST APIs to track hardware requests, replacing manual ticketing with structured tracking and publishing regional runbooks.

**Mentorship & Safety**
Evaluated and certified technicians on data security standards, Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) electrical safety, and hardware servicing. Served as site ergonomics champion for safe material handling during heavy rack installations.

**Cross-Functional Operations**
Coordinate with remote deployment engineers, network operations, and site logistics leads during scheduled maintenance windows, providing clear status handoffs to protect cluster availability SLOs.
